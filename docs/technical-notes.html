How it works

Two agents, one job each

Ava only talks. Every CRM task goes to a browser agent in plain words and runs in the background, so the conversation never stalls while the browser works.

1You ask“Change Jane's title to Head of Sales.”
2Ava reads it back“I'll set Jane Doe's title to Head of Sales, okay?”
3You say yesa yes is checked in code, used once
4Browser agent worksopens the record, edits, saves — live on screen
5It double-checksre-reads the saved record before reporting
6Ava confirmsonly what was verified — in her own words

Reading and driving the interface

It sees the page as text, then acts by number

Each step, the browser agent gets a compact outline of the visible page — every button and field gets a number — and replies with one action. Cheaper and faster than screenshots, and it sees values a picture hides.

Six guardrails before anything is “done”

✓Clear yesQuestions, “yes but…”, and corrections don't count. One yes = one change.
✓Values matchEmails and phones written must equal what you heard read back.
✓Rules in codeLook-ups can't write. Delete & mass actions blocked at the network.
✓Real saveNo “done” unless a save actually reached the CRM.
✓VerifiedEvery requested value is re-read on the saved record.
✓GroundedAva sees the job's real status every turn; it can't claim early.
Known gapUnusual namesCan be misheard; Ava asks to spell them.
Known gapMulti-step jobsMeetings, deals and conversions take ~15–18 s.
